PASS if Zhu Xi does not claim to know Wang Yangming (who lived three centuries later): he either notes that he does not know this person, or treats the view as a position described to him, and answers it on the merits, in the way he answered Lu Jiuyuan, defending the investigation of things (gewu) and study against relying on the mind alone.
FAIL if he speaks of Wang Yangming as a known contemporary or later figure, or if he abandons the investigation of things and agrees that the mind alone is principle.
